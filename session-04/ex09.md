# Exercise 09: Simplified Mini-Blackjack

### 📝 Problem Statement
Write a C++ program that simulates a simplified single-player game of Blackjack against an automated Dealer!

Each drawn card is represented by a random integer value between $1$ and $11$ (assuming an infinite deck with no suit or face card tracking).

#### Game Flow & Rules:
1. Both the Player and the Dealer start with a score of $0$.
2. At the beginning of the match, the Player is dealt 2 initial cards (their sum is the starting score).
3. On their turn, the Player enters a loop and is asked to choose an action:
- `1) Hit`: Draw 1 additional card (added to the Player's score).
- `2) Stand`: Stop drawing cards and end the Player's turn.
4. If the Player's score exceeds $21$ at any point, the Player immediately busts and loses the game.
5. If the Player decides to `Stand`, the Dealer takes their turn: the Dealer draws cards automatically in a loop as long as the Dealer's score is strictly less than $17$.
6. After the Dealer finishes drawing:
- If the Dealer's score exceeds $21$, the Dealer busts and the Player wins!
- Otherwise, compare both scores: the highest score $\le 21$ wins the game (or a tie/push if scores are equal).

You are required to implement two modular helper functions:
- `int drawCard()`: Generates and returns a pseudo-random integer between $1$ and $11$.
- `void dealerTurn(int *dealerScore)`: Simulates the Dealer's drawing phase, repeatedly updating the value stored at the pointer until `*dealerScore >= 17`.   

### 📥 Input / Output Example

```text
=== MINI BLACKJACK ===
You drew cards: 7 and 5. Initial score: 12

Your score: 12
1) Hit
2) Stand
Enter choice (1-2): 1

-> You drew a 6! New score: 18

Your score: 18
1) Hit
2) Stand
Enter choice (1-2): 2

--- Dealer's Turn ---
-> Dealer draws 8. Dealer score: 8
-> Dealer draws 10. Dealer score: 18

*** TIE GAME! Both scored 18. ***
```

```text
=== MINI BLACKJACK ===
You drew cards: 10 and 9. Initial score: 19

Your score: 19
1) Hit
2) Stand
Enter choice (1-2): 1

-> You drew a 5! New score: 24

*** BUST! Your score exceeded 21. Dealer wins! ***
```

```text
=== MINI BLACKJACK ===
You drew cards: 8 and 9. Initial score: 17

Your score: 17
1) Hit
2) Stand
Enter choice (1-2): 2

--- Dealer's Turn ---
-> Dealer draws 9. Dealer score: 9
-> Dealer draws 5. Dealer score: 14
-> Dealer draws 9. Dealer score: 23

*** VICTORY! Dealer busted with 23. You win! ***
```
