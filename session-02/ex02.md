# Exercise 02: Interactive Option Menu

### 📝 Problem Statement
Write a C++ program that presents the user a menu of options and reads their choice (an integer from 1 to 3):
1. Convert to US Dollars (USD)
2. Convert to British Pounds (GBP)
3. Convert to Japanese Yen (JPY)

### 📥 Input / Output Example
```text
Select a currency conversion option:
1) Convert to US Dollars (USD)
2) Convert to British Pounds (GBP)
3) Convert to Japanese Yen (JPY)
Enter choice (1-3): 2

Option selected: Conversion to British Pounds (GBP).

```

```text
Select a currency conversion option:
1) Convert to US Dollars (USD)
2) Convert to British Pounds (GBP)
3) Convert to Japanese Yen (JPY)
Enter choice (1-3): 5

Invalid option selected. Please choose between 1 and 3.
```
